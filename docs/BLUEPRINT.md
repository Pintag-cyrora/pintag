# The Pintag Architecture & Company Blueprint — pointer

The canonical, company-level document — Constitution, North Star, moats,
engineering ladder (L1–L5), product ladder (P1–P5), AI architecture and
philosophy, Never Again rules, Engineering Principles, KPIs, scorecard,
five-year vision, and architecture governance — lives as a published,
versioned page (currently **v4**):

**https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0f3ed5f9-b428-4e36-800e-b3a8c4474d7f**

It is deliberately ONE document. Do not create parallel strategy documents
in this repo; extend or amend the Blueprint through its own governance
chapter (quarterly review; burden of proof on the proposal). This repo's
`ARCHITECTURE.md` is the code-level companion — patterns and canonical
APIs, not strategy.

Two rules from the Blueprint that bind everything in this repository:

1. **Capabilities are certified, never assumed** (Governance §7). Code is
   not evidence; a demonstrated production exercise with recorded results
   is. States: Designed → Built → Verified → Certified.
2. **The architecture should become easier to understand every year, not
   harder.** Applies to the codebase, the product, the AI systems, the
   documentation — and to the Blueprint itself.
